## 1. Creating your account

You don't need to "register" in the traditional sense. The first time you book a class:

1. Open your studio's link (usually shared on Instagram or WhatsApp).
2. Tap **Reserve** on a class.
3. Enter your **phone number** (this is your account identifier).
4. Enter your **name** and **email**.
5. You'll receive a 6-digit code by SMS — enter it.

That's it. Your account is created. No password to remember.

> 💡 Use the same phone number every time — that's how the studio recognizes you.

## 2. Logging in

Two ways:

- **SMS code (recommended)**: enter your phone number, receive a 6-digit code, enter it.
- **Email magic link**: enter your email, receive a link, click it.

Codes and links expire after 15 minutes for your security. You can request a new one anytime.

You'll stay logged in for **24 hours** before being asked to re-authenticate.

## 3. Browsing the calendar

From the studio's home page:

- See the **week view** of available classes.
- Each class shows: class name, instructor, time, and **mats remaining** (e.g. `4 left of 12`).
- Tap a class to see the full description.

A class is **full** when no mats remain — the **Reserve** button becomes greyed out.

> 🕒 Bookings close 15 minutes before class start (your studio may set a different cutoff).

## 4. Booking a class

1. Pick a class with available mats.
2. Tap **Reserve**.
3. Choose how to pay:
   - **Use 1 session from my package** (if you have an active package with sessions remaining).
   - **Buy a single class** (drop-in).
   - **Buy a new package** (and use 1 session from it for this booking).
4. Confirm.

Your seat is **held for 10 minutes** while you pay. If you don't complete payment in time, the seat is released.

## 5. Paying

You'll be redirected to the secure MIPS payment page.

Two methods:

- **MCB Juice** — tap the Juice button, you'll receive a push notification on your phone to approve.
- **Card** — enter your Visa, Mastercard, or Amex details. 3-D Secure may ask for additional verification from your bank.

Once payment succeeds:

- You return to the app.
- Your booking is **CONFIRMED**.
- You receive an SMS and email confirmation within 60 seconds.

> 🔒 We **never** see or store your card number. Payment is handled entirely by MIPS.

## 6. Your bookings

Tap **My bookings** in the menu:

- **Upcoming** — classes you're booked for, sorted by date.
- **Past** — your history.

Each booking shows:
- Class, date, time, instructor, room.
- Your mat number (when relevant).
- The package the session was charged to.

## 7. Your packages and balance

Tap **My packages** to see:

- Each active package: name (e.g. "5-pack Pilates Mat"), sessions remaining, expiry date.
- Total sessions remaining across all packages.

> ⏳ Sessions expire when your package expires. Use them before the expiry date — they cannot be transferred or refunded after expiry.

## 8. Renewing your package

You can renew anytime from **My packages → Renew**.

When you have only **1 session left**, you'll receive an SMS and email with a one-tap renewal link:

> *"You have 1 session left. Tap here to book your next class before it fills up."*

Tap the link → if you have a **saved card**, your package is renewed instantly. Otherwise you'll be taken to the payment screen.

## 9. Cancelling a booking

From **My bookings**, tap the booking → **Cancel**.

Two cases:

- **More than 12 hours before class** (your studio may set a different window):
  - Your session is **returned to your package**.
  - You're refunded if you paid for a drop-in.
- **Less than 12 hours before class**:
  - The session is **consumed** (not returned).
  - No refund.

The cancellation policy is shown on the confirmation screen before you confirm.

> 💡 Plan ahead. Studios rely on accurate bookings to fill mats.

## 10. Notifications you'll receive

| When | What |
|---|---|
| After booking | Confirmation SMS + email |
| 24 hours before class | Email reminder |
| 2 hours before class | SMS reminder |
| When you have 1 session left | "Renew now" SMS + email |
| 7 days before package expiry | Email reminder |
| If the studio cancels your class | SMS + email + automatic refund |

You can mute the 24h/2h reminders in **Account → Preferences**. Booking confirmations and refund notices cannot be disabled — they are transactional.

## 11. Saved cards and one-tap payment

When you pay by card, you can tick **"Save this card for one-tap renewal"**.

- Only an encrypted token is stored — never your card number.
- You can remove a saved card anytime from **Account → Saved cards**.
- One-tap renewal charges your default saved card without redirecting you.

## 12. Your privacy and preferences

From **Account**:

- Update your name, email, language (French / English).
- Toggle SMS reminders, email reminders.
- Manage saved cards.
- **Export your data** — receive a JSON file with all your bookings, payments, and packages.
- **Delete your account** — your personal data is anonymized; financial records retained for legal reasons.

## 13. Help and contact

- For booking questions: contact your studio directly (WhatsApp / phone shown on the studio page).
- For payment problems: email `support@mips.studio` with your booking reference.
- For technical issues: refresh the app, then contact support if it persists.

Enjoy your classes. 🧘
