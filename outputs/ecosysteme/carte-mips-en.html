4 · Fund flows

Where the money goes: today, and the SPIN file option

Decision of 31 October: model A adopted, provided the licence applied for does not require holding merchant funds and no flow (wallets, transfer) goes through a MIPS account. Model B will only be considered for a clear business reason.

A · Gateway — adopted 31 October
  • Fee invoiced, not withheld.
  • One acquirer contract per merchant (own Outlet).
  • No merchant funds held: no trust to reconcile.
  • To confirm: wallets (MyT Money, Juice) and bank transfer.
B · Collection on behalf — not adopted
  • Acquirer contracts to renegotiate (aggregation).
  • Daily reconciliation bank ↔ ledger ↔ merchant entitlements.
  • Shortfall = immediate alert; four-eyes sign-off.
  • BoM requirements not read at source (bom.mu unavailable).
